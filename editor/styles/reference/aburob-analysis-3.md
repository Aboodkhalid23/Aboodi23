## 6. Motion clues (inferred)

- **Highlighter sweep:** the Wikipedia highlight grows from the name to several lines across two consecutive frames (g1 BR r1c2→c3).
- **Sequential pop-on labels:** the ages go from "103 109" to all five.
- **Slide-in:** the birth certificate enters from the right edge (g2 BL r3c3).
- **Push-ins / 2.5D within one artwork:** the lab goes wide → mid → petri dish, with a drop caught in mid-fall. The rat scales up. A wide cell image spans two consecutive cells, suggesting a lateral pan.
- **Mid-transition frames:**
  - headings clipped at the frame edge ("2010", the blurred heart-card title) suggest slide or blur reveals,
  - blank paper with fading figures suggests a dissolve.
- **Chart build:** the axes appear first, then the points and curve. The dashed orange line stops mid-graph, so it is drawn on.
- **Perspective-tilted screens** with a cursor.
- **Walking cutouts;** smoke probably animated.
- **Unknowable:** easing, timing, transition types, SFX, AI video vs keyframed layers.
