## 6. Motion clues
- **Document push-in:** three consecutive frames zoom closer on one page (L g2 r6c4-c6), so a slow push-in of 20 s or more.
- **Highlight sweep:** bars stop mid-line (L g2 r6c1, M g2 r4c2), consistent with a left-to-right sweep. A "|" caret on TheWrap may mean type-on (uncertain).
- **Poster:** word swap plus a larger cutout suggests a scale-up and text replace.
- **Cartoon:** the hand enters from the top and the referee count rises (4 → 8), so it builds.
- **Arc and popup:** an incomplete arc suggests a stroke write-on; the popup bleeding off the edge suggests a slide-in.
- **Transitions:** a dim portrait suggests a fade (L g4 r1c3); defocused frames suggest blur or zoom transitions (L g3 r4c6, g0 r1c1).
- **Unknown from stills:** easing, speed, whether the map tags pop in one by one, flicker, sound sync.
