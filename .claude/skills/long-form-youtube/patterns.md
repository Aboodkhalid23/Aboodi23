# Long-Form Video Pattern Bank

Fill-in structures with one worked example each.

## Cold-Open Patterns (first line, confirms the click)

- **Restate + prove:** "[Title's claim, near-verbatim] — and I can prove it." — *"You're not bad at coding. You're learning it wrong. And I can prove it."*
- **Result-first:** show the outcome, then "here's how" — *"This is the exact spreadsheet that got me out of debt. By the end you'll have your own."*
- **Stakes:** "[N]% of [audience] [fail state] — and it's almost never because [expected reason]." — *"90% of people who start coding quit in a year — and it's almost never because coding is too hard."*
- **Question gap:** pose the exact question the title implied, then promise the answer with a twist — *"So why DOES your bread taste better at restaurants? It's not the oven."*

## Bookend-Loop Openers (close only at the end)

- "There's one question that predicts [outcome] better than [expected predictor]. I'll ask you it at the end."
- "By the end of this video, one number on screen will mean something completely different to you."
- "I'll tell you the exact moment it all changed — but you need the context first."

## Forward References & Micro-Cliffhangers

- "But that's not even the strange part — I'll get to it."
- "Remember this number. It comes back."
- "And this is where [thing from the hook] starts to form — one more ingredient first."
- Chapter out: "...but escaping [this problem] exposes you to something worse."

## Sag-Point Re-Hooks (40–60%)

- **The reveal-half:** pay off part of the bookend loop, immediately open the other half — *"That's the moment. Knowing when it hits is half of it — surviving it is the other half."*
- **The betrayal:** "Here's the part that should make you angry: [the system benefits from your problem]."
- **The escalation:** "Everything so far was the easy version. Here's where it gets real."
- **The callback reward:** pay off the preview flash from 0:20, then open the next loop.

## Chapter-Title Patterns (curiosity gaps, not labels)

- "The [thing] that feels like [good thing]" — *"The Trap That Feels Like Progress"*
- "Why [common solution] still isn't enough"
- "The [timeframe/number] [event]" — *"The Week-Six Collapse"*
- "What nobody does at [stage]"

## Outro / Next-Video Teases (one CTA)

- "Knowing [this video's payoff] is half of it — the other half is [next video's topic]. In this video I [specific promise] →"
- "If you're past [this video's stage], the next question is [next video's question] — I answer it here →"
