# Title & Thumbnail Pattern Bank

Fill-in structures with one worked example each. All numbers in examples are illustrative — real work uses intake numbers only.

## Title Patterns by Formula

### Result-first
- "How I [result] in [timeframe] (without [expected cost])" — *"How I Learned to Draw Faces in 30 Days (Without Art School)"*
- "[Result] on [constraint]: here's exactly how" — *"A Week of Family Dinners on $50: Here's Exactly How"*
- "I [did unusual thing] for [timeframe]. Here's what happened" — *"I Answered Every Customer Email Myself for a Month. Here's What Happened"*

### Stakes / loss
- "This [common thing] is costing you [specific loss]" — *"This Default Setting Is Costing You an Hour of Battery a Day"*
- "Don't [common action] until you see this" — *"Don't Sign a Lease Until You See This"*
- "The [thing] mistake that quietly ruins [outcome]" — *"The Warm-Up Mistake That Quietly Ruins Your Lifts"*

### Curiosity gap
- "The one [thing] nobody tells [audience] about [topic]" — *"The One Clause Nobody Tells First-Time Authors About"*
- "What [group] know about [topic] that you don't" — *"What Restaurant Chefs Know About Knives That You Don't"*
- "Why [surprising observation]" — *"Why the Best Runners Train Slower Than You"*

### Pattern break
- "Everyone does [common thing]. Here's why it's wrong" — *"Everyone Batches Content. Here's Why It's Wrong"*
- "Stop [common practice]. Do this instead" — *"Stop Studying With Flashcards. Do This Instead"*
- "[Believed constraint]? It doesn't [hold]" — *"Think You Need a Niche? You Don't (Yet)"*

### Specific number
- "[N] [things] that [outcome] ([qualifier])" — *"7 Excel Shortcuts That Save Me an Hour a Day (Accountants, Bookmark This)"*
- "[N] [audience] mistakes I see every week" — *"5 First-Apartment Mistakes I See Every Week as a Realtor"*

### Question gap
- "Why does [surprising thing] happen?" — *"Why Does Your Bread Taste Better at Restaurants?"*
- "Is [common belief] actually true?" — *"Is Premium Gas Actually Worth It?"*

## Thumbnail Concept Bank

- **Emotion + object:** face reacting to the video's key object (shocked at a receipt, delighted at a result). Title names the gap; face carries the stakes.
- **Before/after split:** two states, hard vertical divide. Overlay carries the timeframe or cost, not the title's words.
- **The circled detail:** wide shot with the unnamed "thing" circled/arrowed. Works with curiosity-gap titles — the thumbnail points at what the title won't name.
- **Big number card:** the stat as the dominant element, face small/reacting. Pairs with titles that *don't* contain the number.
- **The forbidden/crossed-out:** the common practice with a red X, the alternative held up. Pairs with pattern-break titles.
- **Scale contrast:** tiny cause vs huge effect in one frame ($3 item next to a $3,000 result).

## Overlay Text Pairing Cheats (≤4 words)

- Title has the price → overlay has the duration: *"7 DAYS. ONE TRIP."*
- Title has the how → overlay has the proof: *"REAL RECEIPT"*
- Title asks why → overlay shows the who: *"EVERY BEGINNER"*
- Title names the mistake → overlay names the cost: *"-$200/MO"*
