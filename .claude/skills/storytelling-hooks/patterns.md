# Hook Pattern Bank

Fill-in structures with one worked example each. Swap the bracketed parts for the user's niche; keep the mechanism. Every pattern names its lever(s) so you can stack deliberately.

## Spoken-Line Patterns

### Curiosity gap
- "Most [audience] lose [resource] to [thing] without ever noticing the one [detail] causing it." — *"Most freelancers lose thousands to scope creep without ever noticing the one contract line causing it."*
- "There's a reason [surprising observation] — and it's not [expected explanation]." — *"There's a reason your sourdough never rises on Sundays — and it's not the yeast."*
- "I did [action] every day for [timeframe]. What happened on day [N] changed how I [activity]." — *"I cold-emailed one stranger every day for 90 days. What happened on day 41 changed how I sell."*
- "Nobody talks about what happens after [milestone]." — *"Nobody talks about what happens after you hit 10k followers."*

### Emotional mirror
- "If [private feeling in the audience's exact words], this is for you." — *"If checking your bank app makes your stomach drop, this is for you."*
- "You're not [self-blame the audience carries]. You're [reframe]." — *"You're not lazy. You're running a schedule built for someone else's life."*
- "That feeling when [hyper-specific shared moment]? There's a fix." — *"That feeling when the client says 'quick question' at 6pm Friday? There's a fix."*

### Conflict engine
- "You're doing everything right with [goal] — and one [hidden thing] is quietly capping your results." — *"You're doing everything right with your ads — and one targeting habit is quietly capping your results."*
- "[Common practice] is exactly why you can't [goal]." — *"Meal planning on Sunday is exactly why you quit by Wednesday."*
- "The harder you try to [goal], the worse [outcome] gets. Here's the trap." — *"The harder you try to sleep early, the worse your nights get. Here's the trap."*

### Pattern + surprise
- "Everyone says [common advice]. [Contradiction]." — *"Everyone says post every day. My channel grew when I stopped."*
- "[Impressive result] — and it had nothing to do with [expected cause]." — *"We doubled signups in a month — and it had nothing to do with the product."*
- "I followed [authority/system] perfectly. It failed. Then I broke one rule." — *"I followed the couch-to-5k plan perfectly. It failed. Then I broke one rule."*

### Relatability + qualification (name the audience in the line)
- "If you [audience-defining behavior], stop scrolling." — *"If you run a Shopify store under $10k/month, stop scrolling."*
- "[Audience]: the reason [shared problem] isn't what you think." — *"New managers: the reason your team goes quiet in meetings isn't what you think."*

## Visual First-Frame Patterns (works on mute)

- **Mid-action interrupt:** caught mid-motion — dropping something, wincing at a screen, tearing up a page. Motion at frame one beats a settling-in talking head.
- **The odd object:** an out-of-place item held to camera (a $2 kitchen timer for a productivity video). The mismatch is the gap.
- **Before/after split:** both states visible instantly — messy desk | clean desk, empty pan | plated dish.
- **The receipt/proof shot:** a real number visible — bank screen, analytics graph, scale, invoice.
- **POV of the problem:** the viewer's own view of the pain — a 47-tab browser, a 6am alarm, an inbox at 999+.

## Text-Overlay Patterns (the other half of the spoken line)

- Spoken line names the pain → overlay hints the answer's territory: *"the invoice ≠ your money"*
- Spoken line makes the claim → overlay carries the number: *"day 41"*, *"$3,000 → $0"*
- Spoken line asks the question → overlay names the audience: *"freelance designers"*
- Never a caption of the spoken words.

## Bookend + CTA Closers (by goal)

- Follows: "I post [specific thing] for [audience] every [cadence] — follow so [next pain moment] doesn't catch you."
- Saves: "Save this for the next time [trigger moment]."
- Comments: "Which [item] are you still doing? Tell me below."
- Shares: "Send this to the [person] who needs [the payoff phrase from the hook]."
